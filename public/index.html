How this works
  1. Scan the challenge above with your BIP47-capable wallet and approve it.
  2. Your wallet and this receiver agree on a fresh address privately, off-chain.
  3. You send one ordinary payment. Nothing marks the connection on-chain.
Before you pay: which wallet you use changes what is public

Nothing about your payment appears on the blockchain either way — that is the point of this receiver. The difference is off-chain.

  • A standard Samourai or Ashigaru wallet announces your payment code in the clear when it contacts this shop, so anyone watching can see that you are a customer here. That is how their protocol works, not a choice this shop made.
  • The scan above proves control of your payment code to this shop alone. Only this shop learns you asked for an address.
Normally BIP47 costs two transactions to a new counterparty — a notification transaction to connect, then the real payment. This receiver stays online so the connection can be made directly, and you only pay once.
